FAQ / 06
COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Everything you need to know about the fest, participation, and what to bring.

DesignVerse is a student-led festival celebrating design, creativity, experimentation, and emerging technology. It brings together curious minds across disciplines to explore new ideas, collaborate on rapid prototypes, and showcase experimental projects.

Participation is open to enrolled university, college, and polytechnic students from all academic disciplines—including design, engineering, computer science, architecture, fine arts, and the humanities.

No prior design background is required. The sessions and design sprint are structured for multidisciplinary collaboration, where curiosity, diverse perspectives, and a willingness to explore matter most.

Attendees can participate in hands-on Creative Labs, the fast-paced collaborative Design Sprint, provocation talks and lightning debates in Ideas on Stage, and the student prototype Showcase.

You can register directly using any of the “Register Now” buttons on this page or by filling out the registration card in the Registration section. Admission is complimentary for all verified students.
